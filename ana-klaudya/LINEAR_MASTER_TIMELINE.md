## Editorial rationale

The existing programmed sections outside the central Elegy already account for roughly five minutes of visual time. The central Elegy is the most spatial and contemplative part of the web work; fixing it at approximately **03:20** preserves its hierarchy instead of compressing it to subtitle-reading speed.

A 09:27 master therefore reflects the actual character of ANA KLAUDYA much better than the former 05:58 / 06:48 timing studies. Those shorter files remain only as abandoned compression experiments and must not be described as the canonical work duration.

## Picture-lock rules

- Preserve all canonical Spanish text; do not cut verses to reach the target.
- Do not mechanically accelerate the spoken voice.
- Compression, if later required by a festival, should affect transition holds before it affects poem delivery.
- Eyes: progressive focus only; no pulse, blink or zoom.
- Music should withdraw when voice enters rather than underline every emotional beat.
- Preserve the second **«el calor»** as a structural climax.
- Preserve the air between the two hands in **Tu piel** and the final «No lo atravieso.»; no contact is asserted as an event.
- Preserve the comic silence of **«—Al puchero, Paco. / —Sal.»** and **«—El pan, poeta.»**
- Final question remains unresolved and self-contained inside ANA KLAUDYA.
- **CENICIENTO is not part of this master.**
