## Canonical short synopsis · 50 words

**ANA KLAUDYA** is an interactive poetic and audiovisual work built from seven poems, image, voice and sound. Through partial approaches, it explores the impossibility of containing a presence within a single representation. The journey culminates in an everyday scene where contemplation finally gives way to a shared life.

## Canonical festival synopsis · 100–120 words

**ANA KLAUDYA** is an interactive poetic and audiovisual work that brings together seven poems, voice, image, sound and visual interludes within a single narrative architecture. Each section approaches a presence from a different perspective: gaze, memory, body, photography or language. None is sufficient on its own.

The work advances through fragments that speak to one another until they reveal that its true subject is not the description of a person, but the impossibility of containing that person completely within an image, a poem or a memory. The journey ends by shifting attention from representation toward the concrete, shared experience of everyday life.

## Canonical long synopsis · catalogue / dossier

**ANA KLAUDYA** is an expanded-poetry project combining text, voice, image, sound and digital navigation. Organized as seven poems and a visual interlude, the work develops a journey that begins in contemplation and arrives at the materiality of everyday objects and gestures.

Each section approaches the same core from a different perspective. Eyes, lips, hair, skin, photography, memory and voice function as successive attempts to approach a presence that can never be completely fixed. Rather than constructing a closed portrait, the work shows that every representation is necessarily partial.

Its architecture reproduces that same idea. The individual elements function autonomously while also forming a larger whole based on the accumulation of incomplete approaches. The viewer moves through a system of fragments whose internal relationships are progressively revealed.

The epilogue transforms the logic of contemplation into an experience of coexistence. What begins as image, language and memory ends by expressing itself through objects and gestures shared around a table. **ANA KLAUDYA** thus explores the distance between presence and representation, memory and experience, and between what can be named and what remains beyond the reach of language.

## Localization rule

This is not an adaptation into a different work. It is the same work in an English-language competition edition.

Therefore:

- preserve durations, pauses and reveal order;
- preserve all visual assets and their timing;
- preserve the relation between text and image;
- preserve the final domestic-material turn of the epilogue;
- translate interface copy semantically, not mechanically;
- do not explain symbols inside the work;
- do not reveal in synopsis or interface the specific final discovery of bread, onion, Cotán or *Flowers.* before the viewer reaches it.
