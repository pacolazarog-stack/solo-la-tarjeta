# ANA KLAUDYA · SUBMISSION CHECKLIST 2027

**Reference master:** linear festival version, editorial target **09:27**.  
**Canonical web version:** interactive, open duration.  
**Author / artistic name:** `flag`.

## Priority route

### 1. Weimar Poetry Film Award 2027

**Compatibility:** YES for the 09:27 master, subject to final encoded runtime and current-call verification at submission time.

Prepare:

- final MP4, 1920×1080, H.264, stereo;
- final measured runtime;
- English subtitles;
- `.srt` or dialogue/subtitle file if requested;
- full Spanish poem;
- complete English literary translation;
- short English synopsis;
- artist statement;
- author/director biography;
- stills in high resolution;
- credits sheet;
- AI-use disclosure describing exactly which tools were used and for what;
- rights inventory for photographs, music, voice, generated/assisted visual material and any third-party assets;
- signed authorization from Ana Klaudya for use of her identifiable image in the work and in festival-related exhibition/promotion;
- separate copyright permission from the photographer if the photographs were not made by the author or Ana and the required exploitation rights are not already held.

### 2. Cadence Video Poetry Festival 2027

**Compatibility:** YES in principle for a final master below 10:00. The 09:27 editorial target leaves only a limited safety margin, so credits must be included in the measured total before submission.

Prepare the same core package as Weimar, with particular attention to:

- exact final runtime including opening and end credits;
- English subtitles that remain legible over every visual background;
- concise logline and synopsis;
- 3–5 representative stills;
- transparent AI-use statement;
- proof of rights for all images and audio;
- image authorization from Ana Klaudya.

**Editorial rule:** do not shorten poetry or accelerate the voice merely to remain below 10 minutes. If the rendered master exceeds the current category limit, first reduce non-verbal holds, fades and bridge durations, without altering the canonical text.

### 3. Midwest Video Poetry Fest 2027

**Canonical master compatibility:** NO if the maximum remains 7:00.

Do not use the 05:58 or 06:48 historical subtitle studies as if they were the canonical film. A Midwest submission would require a separately conceived abbreviated edition and should only be produced if it retains artistic integrity.

## Submission gate

Do **not** submit until all four conditions are satisfied:

1. final MP4 exists and has been watched from beginning to end;
2. exact encoded runtime has been measured;
3. image/music/photography rights documentation is complete;
4. the live rules of the target festival have been rechecked on the day of submission.

The definitive running time is always the runtime of the submitted MP4, not the 09:27 editorial planning target.
